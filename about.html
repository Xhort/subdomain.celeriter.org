About Drip Dye

Color made to help you stand out.

Hi, I’m Libby Drake, founder of Drip Dye. I started this brand to turn creativity into bold, affordable clothing that feels personal—not mass-produced.

Why I started

A creative answer to everyone dressing alike

Drip Dye is for teens, young adults, teams, clubs, families, and anyone who wants clothing with more personality. Each shirt is dyed by hand using vivid colors and artistic patterns, so no two finished pieces look exactly the same.

What makes it different

Handmade, expressive, and yours

Instead of basic fast fashion, Drip Dye focuses on limited drops, custom ideas, and pieces made for real moments—from everyday style to birthdays, school events, sports teams, clubs, and gifts.

The vision

Confidence through color

The goal is to build a recognizable creative brand around confidence, individuality, quality, and connection. As Drip Dye grows, I plan to expand designs, serve more group orders, and bring the collection to local markets and events.
